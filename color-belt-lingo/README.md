# Language Belt Master

I am building an app to learn any language, regardless of what language you currently speak.  You select what you speak and what you want to learn.  As the user completes different modules of increasing difficulty, they earn a "Belt" like in karate.  They start off as a white belt, learn some basic things, pass some tests, and earn their yellow belt, and so on.  My app shows the text of both languages in colors so the corresponding words are the same color.  I also have a chatbot "Friend" they can interact with that will ask them questions.  I was thinking of branding the language something like "Language Ninja" or "Talk Ninja", something catchy and unique.  Earning belts gives a sense of progress and accomplishment.  The app plays audio of male and female voices when doing the program.  The words the app uses come from google sheets and is configurable so a user can add their own words and the app will pick it up and use it in training.  I have the Monthly and Lifetime Subscriptions available.  A database keeps track of user's progress and steers them through the pipeline.  My current app uses shades of blue which I like.
